A year through the New Testament — studying Scripture through the lens of what it means to love God, love people, and make disciples as men.

The Universal Calling — For Every Person

Love God. Love People. Make Disciples.

God's call to every man, woman, and child on earth — the Great Commandment and Great Commission.

1
Love God Pursue God wholeheartedly in devotion, character & daily walk
Matthew 22:37
"You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind."
2
Love People Serve and sacrificially love those around you as Christ loved us
Matthew 22:39
"You shall love your neighbor as yourself."
3
Make Disciples Every believer is called to invest in others and multiply the faith
Matthew 28:19
"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations."

As men, we answer this calling in a unique way

The Man's Specific Call

As Men, We Are Called to
Love, Provide & Protect

The Great Commandment and Great Commission lived out in the heart of men.

01
Love
A man leads his wife, his home, his friendships, and his community the same way Christ loved the church — sacrificially, consistently, and without condition.
John 15:12
"Love one another as I have loved you."
02
Provide
Provision is more than a paycheck. A man provides spiritually, emotionally, and physically for those God has placed in his care.
1 Timothy 5:8
"If anyone does not provide for his relatives… he has denied the faith."
03
Protect
A man stands in the gap — guarding his family from spiritual danger, cultural drift, and anything that would pull them away from God.
Nehemiah 4:14
"Fight for your brothers, your sons, your daughters, your wives."
